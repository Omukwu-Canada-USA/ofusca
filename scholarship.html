2026 Scholarship Award

Applications are open from 1 August to 1 December 2026 for undergraduate students from Omukwu in tertiary institutions in Nigeria.

2026-27 academic year

5th annual OFUSCA Scholarship Award

The Omukwu Family USA and Canada (OFUSCA) is offering its 5th annual scholarship award for the 2026-27 academic school year to undergraduate students in tertiary institutions in Nigeria.

  • Applications open: 1 August 2026
  • Deadline: 1 December 2026
  • Who can apply: undergraduate students of Omukwu origin in Nigerian tertiary institutions
Apply online Download the application form (PDF) Email your application
Eligibility criteria

All applicants

  1. Must be a native of Omukwu.
  2. Must submit the scholarship application on or before the deadline.

Incoming students (freshmen)

  1. Minimum of 5 credits in WAEC/NECO or equivalent.
  2. Enrolled in a tertiary institution.

Returning students

  1. Must be attending a tertiary institution in the 2026/27 session.
  2. Must demonstrate above-average academic performance in the 2025 session.
How to apply

Five steps to submit your application

  1. Apply online on the online application page, or download and print the application form above.
  2. Complete Section 1 (all applicants), then Section 2 if you are an incoming student or Section 3 if you are a returning student.
  3. Attach your documents. Incoming students: a photocopy of your certificates or statement of results, and your course registration form. Returning students: the Enrolment Verification form (page 2 of the application), completed by you and signed and stamped by your Head of Department.
  4. Sign and date the application form.
  5. If you used the paper form, submit it to Professor D. O. Dike by email at ofusca.scholar@gmail.com or by hand, on or before 1 December 2026.
What the form asks for

Have this information ready

So you can fill the form in one sitting.

Section 1: All applicants

  • Surname and given names
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address
  • Name of your kindred in Omukwu

Section 2: Incoming students

  • Tertiary institution you will attend in 2026-27
  • Course of study admitted, and mode of study (part-time or full-time)
  • Secondary school attended, year started and completed, and certificate obtained
  • Your best 5 subjects in WAEC or NECO, with grades and year taken

Section 3: Returning students

  • Tertiary institution and course of study
  • Year started and current year of study
  • Your 2025/26 GPA
  • Part-time or full-time study

Enrolment Verification (returning students)

  • You fill in: name, year of study, school, department, session and semester
  • Your Head of Department confirms your enrolment (full-time or part-time) and current GPA
  • Head of Department's name, title, date, phone number, signature and office stamp
Questions about the scholarship? Email ofusca.scholar@gmail.com. Remember to include all required attachments and submit on or before 1 December 2026.
