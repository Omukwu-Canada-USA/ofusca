About OFUSCA

Omukwu Family USA/Canada Association exists to gather Omukwu indigenes and families abroad around a shared heritage, shared responsibility, and shared future.

Mission

Brotherhood, unity, peace, development, and cultural continuity.

OFUSCA’s mission is to promote togetherness, stimulate love, care for one another, encourage family harmony, integrate individual and community empowerment, and entrust the organization to the guidance and wisdom of God.

The association supports Omukwu people in the USA and Canada while maintaining a living connection to Omukwu at home.

Core objectives

  1. Create opportunities to meet, exchange ideas, and foster growth, unity, and peace.
  2. Preserve and instill cultural heritage among the youth.
  3. Encourage educational growth, skill acquisition, political empowerment, and social-cultural mix.
  4. Establish branches wherever Omukwu indigenes reside.
  5. Organize fundraising conventions for Omukwu development and diaspora support.
