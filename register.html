Why this form matters

A stronger member database for unity, welfare, birthdays, and publicity.

OFUSCA’s constitutional vision includes unity, cultural preservation, education, empowerment, and care for one another. This online profile helps the association know its members better and communicate with dignity and accuracy.

  • Birthday and anniversary recognition
  • Member spotlight and professional/business publicity
  • Scholarship, youth mentorship, volunteer and donor coordination
  • Branch, family, welfare and emergency contact coordination
  • Accurate dues, registration, and good-standing follow-up

Privacy note: Full date of birth and family details should be used for administrative records only. Public birthday posts should use month/day unless the member gives additional consent.
