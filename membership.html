Membership

Join, participate, support, and remain connected to the Omukwu family across the USA and Canada.

Who may join?

Membership is open to persons of Omukwu descent either by birth or legal adoption. Children of Omukwu indigenes become eligible for individual membership on their 18th birthday, if they desire.

Online membership system

  • Register as a new member or update your existing profile
  • Submit birthday, profession, family, branch, and contact information
  • Give consent for birthday celebrations, flash news, member spotlight, and business promotion
  • Submit payment confirmation for registration, dues, donations, levies, and event payments
  • Support scholarship, youth mentorship, welfare, and community development projects

Membership record priorities

Identity

Celebration
Birthday month/day, family milestones, achievements.
Profession
Occupation, business, expertise, links and member spotlight information.
Consent
Permission before public birthday, publicity or website use.

The public website should only show information members have approved for publicity.
