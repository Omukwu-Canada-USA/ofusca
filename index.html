Who we are

One association, one heritage, one shared responsibility.

Omukwu Family USA/Canada Association is a family association of brothers, sisters, married people, sons, daughters, and adopted members who share common heritage and believe in the progress of Omukwu Community at home and abroad.

The organization is founded on brotherhood, family harmony, community empowerment, love, care for one another, and trust in the guidance and wisdom of God.

Membership

Membership is open to Omukwu descendants by birth or legal adoption.

OFUSCA welcomes persons of Omukwu descent and families who desire to join, participate, and support the mission of unity, peace, cultural preservation, and development.

Register as a MemberPay Dues or Donate

Membership Pathway

  1. Complete registration as a person or family.
  2. Connect with your local state/provincial branch or zone.
  3. Pay applicable dues or levy according to official guidance.
  4. Participate in meetings, events, committees, and community projects.
  5. Remain in good standing to enjoy member benefits.

Constitution

The uploaded constitution outlines OFUSCA’s mission, objectives, membership, dues, meetings, offices, conduct, benefits, social activities, and community projects.

Executive officers are elected by the general membership, and major executive decisions require approval of the majority of members.
